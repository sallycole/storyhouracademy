Tests

Use these tests for diagnostic information. Offer a pretest when students haven't yet been taught all the material. Offer a mastery test when students have been taught all the material. The tests are the same, but students will feel less pressure to get everything correct in a pretest and you can reassure them that there's no penalty for wrong answers.
